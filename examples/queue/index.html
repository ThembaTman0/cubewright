A queue only absorbs a burst; it can't fix a producer that is faster than its consumer. The press handles 1.2 items a second. Below that arrival rate the belt drains. Above it, the belt fills to its limit, the red lamp tells the producer to wait, and the pile grows back at the source. That's backpressure. When the source runs out of room too, work is dropped.
