Each colour is a key. Hits are served straight from the shelf, and the key moves to the front because it was used most recently. A miss makes a slow trip to the warehouse; the fetched key goes in front, and the least recently used key at the far end falls off. Popular keys stay, so the hit rate is high. A scan of many different keys pushes them all out, and the cache thrashes.
