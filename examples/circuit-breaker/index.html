When a service goes down, every request to it hangs until it times out, which ties up callers and piles more load on a struggling system. After three failures in a row the breaker trips: the bar drops, and requests fail instantly instead of waiting. After a cooldown it goes half-open and lets one trial request through. If the trial succeeds, the bar lifts. If it fails, the bar drops again.
